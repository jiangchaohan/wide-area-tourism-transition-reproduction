# Data provenance and processing boundaries

- Source type: route-product records obtained from a culture-and-tourism administrative information system.
- Raw records: 36,347.
- Exact duplicate records removed: 22.
- Routes excluded because fewer than two attraction states remained after cleaning: 3,056.
- Modeling routes: 33,269.
- Original attraction nodes: 1,049.
- Standardized modeling states: 1,034.
- Adjacent directed transitions: 736,897 across 16,555 distinct edges.

The records describe planned or registered route products, not real-time individual trajectories. The route texts contain no direct personal identifiers in the supplied modeling tables. Source-language attraction names and route descriptions remain in Chinese to avoid altering the empirical evidence.
